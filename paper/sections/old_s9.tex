\section{Related Work and Discussion}
\label{sec:related}

\citet{Joldes2017} derived tight bounds for the principal double-word building
blocks.  \citet{Rideau2022} subsequently mechanized these analyses in
Rocq/Flocq, correcting a published multiplication bound in the process.  Their
mechanized results provide the principal
mathematical foundation reused here.  This work concentrates on the passage to
bounded IEEE~754 computations and on connecting the resulting conditional
theorems to checked C interfaces.

Flocq provides floating-point semantics and reusable rounding results
\cite{Boldo2011}.  Earlier work has mechanized rounding-error arguments and
expansion renormalization~\cite{Roux2014,Boldo2015}.  VST and CompCert provide
the program logic and compiler connection used here to carry numerical
postconditions to C callers~\cite{Appel2014}.

\citet{Zhang2025} automatically verify floating-point accumulation networks and
synthesize an addition network.  Their adjacent-term
nonoverlap relation specializes, for two components, to the rounding relation
used for double words here.  The comparison dimensions relevant to this paper
are the arithmetic model, numerical guarantee, treatment of finite exponent
ranges, executable hypotheses, program-level refinement, and the interface
property made available to clients.

QD is a reference implementation of double-double and quad-double arithmetic
\cite{QD}.  It prioritizes portable numerical performance and provides
multiple algorithmic variants.  We use it as a performance and behavioral
baseline while keeping the contract comparison explicit: the distinguishing
question is which preconditions and postconditions are checked or proved at the
call boundary.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Runtime checks may be sufficient without being necessary and can therefore
reject inputs for which a particular execution would have an acceptable
result.  They also impose a runtime cost.  The current contracts assume the
stated binary64 rounding and FMA behavior and the toolchain versions recorded
by the artifact.  Extending the interface to other formats, rounding modes, or
failure policies requires corresponding models, condition bridges, and program
proofs.  The exact guarantee and closure property must be read per exported
operation rather than inferred from the library name.
