\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper organizes a verified double-word library around the connection
between conditional numerical theorems and callable C interfaces.  A verified
runtime check supplies an operation condition, VST relates the C kernel to its
Rocq model and composes the guarded control flow, and the numerical development
supplies the applicable result for a normally returning call.  The explicit
pipeline clarifies both the guarantee available to a caller and the boundary of
the end-to-end claim.
